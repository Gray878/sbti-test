## How the SBTI test is structured

- The test uses **15 dimensions** across self, emotion, attitude, action, and social style.
- There are **30 regular questions** plus **2 special questions**.
- The result library contains **27 personalities**.
- **DRUNK** is a hidden type triggered by a special drinking path.
- **HHHH** is a fallback type used when the standard library cannot match the answer pattern well enough.

## The 27 personality types

### CTRL | Controller
<img src="/sbti/images/CTRL.png" alt="CTRL poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: control, execution, structure.
- Summary: CTRL types naturally take over messy situations and turn chaos into process. They tend to behave like a human reset button for disorganized lives.

### ATM-er | The Giver
<img src="/sbti/images/ATM-er.png" alt="ATM-er poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: reliable, self-sacrificing, always covering the bill.
- Summary: ATM-er does not just “pay” in money. This type often pays with time, energy, patience, and emotional labor, which makes them dependable but also easy to overuse.

### Dior-s | The Loser-Sage
<img src="/sbti/images/Dior-s.jpg" alt="Dior-s poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: low desire, anti-hustle, realistic detachment.
- Summary: Dior-s rejects empty ambition theater. This type often sees through status games early and would rather stay comfortable and clear-eyed than perform fake self-improvement.

### BOSS | Leader
<img src="/sbti/images/BOSS.png" alt="BOSS poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: direction, pressure, upward force.
- Summary: BOSS personalities like to hold the wheel. They care about results, pace, and order, and they often move through life as if everything is a project that needs a stronger operator.

### THAN-K | The Thankful One
<img src="/sbti/images/THAN-K.png" alt="THAN-K poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: optimism, warmth, recovery.
- Summary: THAN-K tends to salvage meaning from bad situations. They are not naive so much as naturally oriented toward gratitude, softness, and emotional renewal.

### OH-NO | Disaster Preventer
<img src="/sbti/images/OH-NO.png" alt="OH-NO poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: risk awareness, boundaries, prevention.
- Summary: OH-NO types mentally run failure scenarios before others even notice danger. They often look cautious, but their caution is exactly what keeps systems from collapsing.

### GOGO | The Doer
<img src="/sbti/images/GOGO.png" alt="GOGO poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: action first, discussion second.
- Summary: GOGO prefers momentum over reflection. This type hates leaving things in planning limbo and usually feels best when tasks are moving, closing, and disappearing.

### SEXY | The Magnetic One
<img src="/sbti/images/SEXY.png" alt="SEXY poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: presence, allure, attention gravity.
- Summary: SEXY is less about literal beauty and more about undeniable presence. They tend to pull focus without trying very hard.

### LOVE-R | Romantic Maximalist
<img src="/sbti/images/LOVE-R.png" alt="LOVE-R poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: emotional intensity, idealism, deep attachment.
- Summary: LOVE-R experiences feelings at full volume. Ordinary moments easily become emotionally symbolic, and relationships are often treated with unusual seriousness.

### MUM | The Mother
<img src="/sbti/images/MUM.png" alt="MUM poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: empathy, soothing, emotional caregiving.
- Summary: MUM is good at noticing what others need and stepping in gently. The weakness is that this kindness often gets distributed outward long before it reaches the self.

### FAKE | Mask Shifter
<img src="/sbti/images/FAKE.png" alt="FAKE poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: adaptation, performance, layered identity.
- Summary: FAKE can switch modes quickly depending on context. That flexibility is socially powerful, but it can also blur where performance ends and the “real self” begins.

### OJBK | Whatever Person
<img src="/sbti/images/OJBK.png" alt="OJBK poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: low conflict, low insistence, easygoing decisions.
- Summary: OJBK is not necessarily empty of preference. They simply refuse to waste dramatic energy on choices that do not matter much.

### MALO | Monkey Brain Trickster
<img src="/sbti/images/MALO.png" alt="MALO poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: playfulness, weird ideas, anti-formality.
- Summary: MALO treats life like a side quest full of jumps, detours, and jokes. This type dislikes total behavioral domestication and often finds fun where structure fails.

### JOKE-R | Clown
<img src="/sbti/images/JOKE-R.jpg" alt="JOKE-R poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: humor, atmosphere, hidden emptiness.
- Summary: JOKE-R keeps rooms alive by turning pain into performance. Their jokes can be both social talent and emotional armor.

### WOC! | The “Whoa” Person
<img src="/sbti/images/WOC.png" alt="WOC poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: loud reaction, quiet judgment.
- Summary: WOC! may look exaggerated on the surface, but the inside is often more measured than it seems. The dramatic outburst is usually their final comment, not the start of intervention.

### THIN-K | Thinker
<img src="/sbti/images/THIN-K.png" alt="THIN-K poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: logic, analysis, cognitive distance.
- Summary: THIN-K likes to check claims, motives, evidence, and hidden bias. Their silence often means internal review, not absence.

### SHIT | Bitter World-Saver
<img src="/sbti/images/SHIT.png" alt="SHIT poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: contempt outside, responsibility underneath.
- Summary: SHIT complains about everything yet still cleans up disasters. The contradiction is the point: irritation does not stop them from carrying weight.

### ZZZZ | The Deadliner
<img src="/sbti/images/ZZZZ.png" alt="ZZZZ poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: delayed activation, emergency awakening.
- Summary: ZZZZ may look absent until the deadline becomes real. Once pressure spikes, they often wake up all at once.

### POOR | The Narrow Beam
<img src="/sbti/images/POOR.png" alt="POOR poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: focus, selective energy, hard prioritization.
- Summary: POOR is “poor” only in the sense that attention gets concentrated instead of spread. This type can ignore almost everything except the one target they truly care about.

### MONK | Monk
<img src="/sbti/images/MONK.png" alt="MONK poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: distance, privacy, sacred personal space.
- Summary: MONK values separation and calm. They are not cold by accident; they simply believe intimacy must never erase independence.

### IMSB | The Self-Defeating Fool
<img src="/sbti/images/IMSB.png" alt="IMSB poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: impulse fighting insecurity.
- Summary: IMSB often swings between “go for it” and “you will embarrass yourself.” What looks like hesitation is usually an internal civil war.

### SOLO | The Isolated One
<img src="/sbti/images/SOLO.png" alt="SOLO poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: low self-worth, defensive distance, hidden sensitivity.
- Summary: SOLO protects the self by backing away first. Their harsh shell often covers a strong fear of rejection and abandonment.

### FUCK | Wild Force
<img src="/sbti/images/FUCK.png" alt="FUCK poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: raw vitality, anti-domestication, instinct.
- Summary: FUCK rejects polite containment. This type values intensity, freedom, and living force more than socially approved behavior.

### DEAD | The Exhausted Sage
<img src="/sbti/images/DEAD.png" alt="DEAD poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: low desire, low thrill, post-meaning fatigue.
- Summary: DEAD feels like someone who has already emotionally finished the game. They often move with a detached sense that many ordinary goals are no longer worth much.

### IMFW | Fragile Believer
<img src="/sbti/images/IMFW.png" alt="IMFW poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: sensitivity, dependence, low emotional armor.
- Summary: IMFW tends to trust deeply once safety is felt. Their vulnerability often comes not from incompetence but from being too sincere and too easy to reach.

### HHHH | Fallback Laugher
<img src="/sbti/images/HHHH.png" alt="HHHH poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: system fallback, mismatch result.
- Summary: HHHH is less a normal type than a sign that the answer pattern resisted clean classification. It is the test admitting that the standard library ran out of confidence.

### DRUNK | Drunkard
<img src="/sbti/images/DRUNK.png" alt="DRUNK poster" width="220" />

- Core vibe: hidden route, alcohol-triggered chaos.
- Summary: DRUNK is the test’s hidden joke result. It is activated through the special drinking path and exists more as a dramatic easter egg than a standard personality profile.

## Why this test spread so fast

- **The names are brutal:** they sound like internet nicknames, not textbook categories.
- **The writing is theatrical:** each result reads like a character monologue rather than a clinical summary.
- **The mechanics feel game-like:** hidden questions, secret outcomes, fallback types, and posters all increase shareability.
