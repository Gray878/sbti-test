# About SBTI Personality Test

SBTI is an entertainment-focused personality test based on 15 dimensions, featuring 30 regular questions and 2 special questions, with a total of 27 personality types. Unlike traditional MBTI, SBTI uses internet-native Chinese naming conventions that resonate more with younger generations.

## Test Features

- **15 Core Dimensions**: Covering multiple aspects including self, emotion, attitude, action, and social interaction
- **27 Personality Types**: From CTRL (Controller) to DEAD (Dead), each personality has unique characteristics
- **Hidden Mechanisms**: Includes specially triggered hidden personality DRUNK and system fallback personality HHHH
- **Visual Results**: Each personality comes with exclusive posters and detailed interpretations
- **Entertainment-Oriented**: Uses internet meme culture and slang naming, highly memorable and easy to spread
